# Steam: the patrol boat's crew on a client. In about half of the longer runs (2026-10-30) the
# client logged "SetParams(pe_params_pos): (Merc_Cover.MercCover_Falcon_1 ...) Validation Error:
# pos is invalid" every frame from the map start: the boat's gunner stayed at its level spot
# (240 m from the server's, which sits in the boat) and got an invalid position each frame (the
# fake entering jump divides by the time to the seat). In 3 runs the flood ended in Lua "stack
# overflow" all over the client scripts and the client dropped out after 25-80 s.
# Fixed 2026-11-12 (COOP/ClientStuff.lua CoopWrapClientSeat): 12 of 17 red before, 6 of 6 green after.
# Run from the repo root: tools/bin/harness.exe test tests/steam-boat-crew.txt
up -mode host -map steam -clients 1 -mod FarCryCoop -asi tools/bin/FarCryCoopNet.asi -start #COOP_ExpectPlayers=2;#CoopMap("steam")
wait server players>=2 -t 90s
wait client1 players>=2 -t 120s
sleep 60s
# the client is still in the game
wait client1 players>=2 -t 10s
# the crew sits in the boat on both sides (the boat itself may lag while it moves)
expect-diff max=8 class=^(MercCover|MercScout)$
# the gunner sits on the client too: the server sends the sit animation once, when it seats the crew
# at the map start, before the client is in (2026-11-12: the client's gunner stood in the boat)
expect-log client1 anim.Merc_Cover.MercCover_Falcon_1.gunboat_gunner_sit
expect-no-events
down
